The theory was incomplete in three specific ways, all now fixed.

51. ASSUMPTIONS WERE NOT IN THE STATEMENTS. Proposition 1 read "let f be exactly
    O(3)-equivariant ... then f(x) = 0", but the two assumptions the proof actually uses,
    permutation invariance and translation invariance, appeared only in the surrounding
    prose. A theorem whose hypotheses live outside it is not a theorem. (A1), (A2) and (A3)
    are now stated in the main body and named in every statement that uses them.

52. A NOVEL RESULT WAS ONLY IN THE APPENDIX. The Jacobian identity J o P = -J, which is
    the theoretical basis for Figure 5b and for the claim that the O(3) guarantee is
    differentiable, was proved in the Supplementary Information and never stated in the
    main text. It is now Corollary 2 in the main body, with its proof in the appendix.

53. "MACHINE ZERO" HAD NO THEOREM BEHIND IT. The paper asserted throughout that the O(3)
    arms sit at a "float32 noise floor", and observed that MACE's floor is ~8x the others'
    because its symmetric contractions stay in single precision. That was an anecdote.
    It is now Proposition 1 (Arithmetic floor):

        ||f(x)||  <=  (delta + eta) / 2

    where delta is the defect in permutation/translation invariance at x and eta the defect
    in O(3)-equivariance at Q = I. Two lines to prove, and it does three jobs: it recovers
    the structural zero when delta = eta = 0; it shows the residual floor is set by the
    ARITHMETIC and not by the symmetry, which is exactly what the data show; and for a
    parity-blind model Lemma 1 forces eta = 2||f(x)||, so the bound collapses to an identity
    and constrains nothing. The guarantee and its stability are lost together.

54. RESTRUCTURED AROUND ONE ENGINE. All four results are consequences of a single identity,
    which is now stated first and separately as Lemma 1 (Inversion identity): for a
    centrosymmetric crystal, f(I.x) = f(x) for ANY permutation- and translation-invariant
    model, parity or no parity. Theorem 1 is Lemma 1 plus O(3)-equivariance. Corollary 1
    (test-time symmetrization is vacuous) is Lemma 1 ALONE. Corollary 2 is Lemma 1
    differentiated. Corollary 3 is the rotation-subgroup analogue. The paper previously
    stated three of these in prose, in three different sections, without noticing they were
    the same identity.

55. ADDED A STABILITY CLAUSE TO COROLLARY 3, which turns the EquiformerV2 anomaly into a
    prediction. If (A1)-(A2) hold only to an absolute defect delta and (A3) only to a
    relative defect epsilon < 1, then on a crystal whose rotation subgroup admits no
    invariant, ||f(x)|| <= delta/(1 - epsilon). For the three matched SO(3) cores, delta and
    epsilon sit at the arithmetic floor, the bound is ~1e-6, four orders below the operating
    threshold, and they flag NONE of the 166 m-3m crystals in any seed. EquiformerV2 has
    both of order 1e-1, the bound is weak, and it flags 53.4% of them. The theory now
    explains why the ceiling is a ceiling only for exactly equivariant models.

56. Both bounds were verified numerically before being written into the paper, and the

== ONE REAL SCIENTIFIC ERROR, FOUND AND FIXED ==
62. THE HEADLINE COUNT WAS WRONG. The paper said "nine of fifteen architectures cannot
    structurally forbid such predictions". That overstates the case for the three
    VECTOR-ONLY models (PaiNN, TorchMD-Net, ViSNet). Their single Cartesian vector channel
    is built from edge vectors r_ij, so under inversion it flips sign: it is an implicitly
    POLAR (odd) vector, exactly as ICTP's Cartesian harmonics are, and the paper already
    classified ICTP as parity-aware on precisely that ground. A rank-3 head assembled from
    an ODD number of vector factors on such a backbone would therefore be parity-odd and
    would vanish structurally on a centrosymmetric crystal. I verified this numerically.

    The paper's own Table 1 consequence column was already careful ("no typed irreducible
    representations; a rank-3 head requires extra machinery"). It was the SUMMARY that
    overstated. A PaiNN or ViSNet author refereeing this paper would have caught it.

    Corrected everywhere (abstract, intro, Results, Table 1, Discussion, cover letter) to
    what is strictly true and is in fact a sharper claim:
      * six of fifteen TYPE parity in their features, so the zero is structural;
      * three are ROTATION-ONLY: parity is absent from the features and NO readout can
        restore it. This is the group the paper measures, and it is the group beyond repair;
      * three are VECTOR-ONLY: implicitly polar but untyped, with no rank-3 path, so any
        guarantee is the practitioner's to build and nothing checks it;
      * three are INVARIANT and cannot express an equivariant tensor output at all.
    Red flag 10 asks the authors to run their own reflection test on the vector-only rows,
    which their protocol should have covered and did not.

== FOUR PHYSICS / MATHEMATICS CORRECTIONS ==
63. NON-SYMMORPHIC SPACE GROUPS. Corollary 3's proof said the rotated structure "coincides
    with x up to an atom permutation and lattice translations". For a screw axis or a glide
    plane the point-group rotation is paired with a FRACTIONAL translation, which is not a
    lattice vector. The proof survives, because (A2) covers rigid shifts by any vector, but
    the statement of (A2) and the proof both had to say so. A crystallography referee would
    have caught this in the first read.
64. SHG DOES NOT VANISH EXACTLY. The paper claimed the second-harmonic-generation tensor
    "vanishes identically in every centrosymmetric crystal". True only in the ELECTRIC-DIPOLE
    approximation: quadrupole and surface contributions give SHG in centrosymmetric media,
    and this is well documented. Qualifier added in both places.
65. THE EQUIVARIANCE DEFECT WAS ILL-POSED. Proposition 1 defined eta as "the defect in (A3)
    at Q = I". An SO(3)-labelled model never asserts (A3) at Q = I, so it has no defect there.
    Recast as a MEASURED failure of the O(3) law, which is what it is.
66. THE INVERSION POINT. The experiments invert about the CELL ORIGIN; Definition 1 places
    the inversion centre at the origin. These coincide only in a standard setting. Translation
    invariance bridges them, and the proof now says so. Also added the frozen-neighbour-graph
    hypothesis to Corollary 2, which the measurement uses and the proof needs.
